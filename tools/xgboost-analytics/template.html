How to read this report

The scenario is processed once, in chronological order. Each payment is scored before its history update. All transfers continue in observed-history mode, including decisions marked blocked. Outcome labels are joined after scoring and do not tune the model or its threshold.

Probability is the model’s uncalibrated estimate. The policy uses bits: −log₂(1 − probability). A payment is blocked only when its score is greater than its original threshold. Warm-up payments are allowed and excluded from classification metrics and the block-rate denominator.
